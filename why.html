why i made this site

somehow, despite it all, i still care.

open your eyes and look around

the present is terrifying. the future is even more so.

the planet is dying. populist far-right political sentiment is rising around the world, and they love a good scapegoat. the world is becoming more hostile, less open, less free.
AI is going to kill us all, and if it doesn't, it's going to forever bring an end to social mobility. nobody is coming to save you; everybody is too busy trying to become part of the techoaristocracy.
we are surrounded by ever increasing surveillance and government corruption. we all know that our phones spy on us, but it's not just the phones anymore. interconnected camera networks spanning the country watch your every move. every purchase you have ever made, every city you have ever been, every person you have ever talked to... it's sitting in a database somewhere, just waiting...
the elimination of consensus reality started by exploitative algorithms is being finalized by indistinguishable deepfakes and a deluge of slop. do you really still think that everybody you talk to online is real?
the incomprehensible web of systems that keep our modern world possible are fragile, and we've now all had a peek at what happens when they're pushed to their limits. what if they collapse?

but somehow, despite it all, i still have hope.

somehow, despite it all, i still care. i believe that a better world is possible.

the world has always been on fire. shit has always sucked.
i'd like to do whatever i can to push things in the direction of being a little bit less terrible.

you can just do things. reach heaven by violence, one star at a time.

what this site is

You are a leviathan of butterflies' butterfly effects. You can't discern your reach.

this site is one of many ways that i'd like to work towards making the world a bit less terrible.

i'd like to make my ideas more legible, in the hope that doing so could facilitate their spread. i am ultimately limited in what i can accomplish on my own, but i hope that if i package my ideas well enough, they might have a better shot at reaching the right people.

however, this site won't just be about be sharing my own thoughts; it's also going to be a platform i'd like to use to amplify works that have deeply impacted how i view the world.
