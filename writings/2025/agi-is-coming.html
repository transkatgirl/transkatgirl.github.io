AGI is coming, and we can't keep pretending like it's not

🚧 this page is a work in progress!

there's this common view in left-leaning circles that all the developments going on in generative AI is nothing more than a big grift. the idea is that we're essentially wasting enormous sums of money on an endeavour that will never bear any fruit, besides maybe burning the commons to the ground.

well, they're half-right here.

we are in a generative AI bubble with obvious parallels to the dotcom bubble, such as vendor financing, unsustainable rates of spending, and unrealistic valuations for unprofitable companies.

and, generative AI is filling the commons with slop that is becoming indistinguishable from reality, destroying the incentives to create internet content, making academic dishonesty easier than ever, and weakening our ability to think for ourselves.

and yet, i don't believe it's all a grift.
i believe there's something more here besides just burning the commons to the ground in order to make a bit of money.

and i believe what's coming next is terrifying.

science-fiction has always talked about the risks of future AI systems with cognitive abilities comparable to or exceeding that of humans. in the field of machine learning, this hypothetical future AI system is referred to as Artificial General Intelligence (AGI).

and despite all the limitations of our current generative AI systems, they've shown us that AGI isn't as far away as we think.
