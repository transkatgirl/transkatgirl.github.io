people are not fungible

🚧 this page is a work in progress!

many aspects of our society tend to treat individuals as fungible and replaceable units, differentiated by only a small set of attributes.

employers view employees as replaceable units that can be exchanged for another employee with a similar skillset. "job security" is a thing of the past for most.

dating apps only differentiate individuals by a few factors like height, weight, and appearance. this isn't an accident, it's a result of revealed preferences.

slowly, more and more aspects of relationships (both platonic and romantic) are becoming transactional, as we begin to view people more and more not as an end, but as a means to an end.
