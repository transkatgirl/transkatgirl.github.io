bodily autonomy is a human right

🚧 this page is a work in progress!

the one thing in common between lgbtq+ rights, abortion, medical assistance in dying, and drug legalization is bodily autonomy.

one side believes that bodily autonomy is a human right. the other side believes that bodily autonomy should be restricted in order to prevent society from being plagued by the issues it creates.

i believe that the case for restricting bodily autonomy is incredibly flimsy. most of the time, it's actually just a difference in cultural values being justified by greatly exaggerating or even fabricating the harms and greatly underplaying the benefits.

in my ideal world, a country limiting the bodily autonomy of its citizens would be viewed the same way as a country limiting what its citizens can say. i believe it should be viewed as a self-evident, nonnegotiable human right.

however, i know this isn't enough to change people's minds. so instead, i've tried my best to put together a brief overview of the real costs and benefits of doing so.[1]
