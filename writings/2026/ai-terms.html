my ontology of AI progress

written 2026-11-27

AI progress is advancing extremely fast and is hard to measure well, and a lot of the words we use to describe future AI systems are nebulous.

this creates the perfect storm for constantly moving goalposts and a shepard's tone -like illusion of constant advancement without real progress.

in order to prevent this, i try to ground commonly used terms in real, measurable outcomes and stick to them. others may use these terms slightly differently, but this is my personal ontology of AI progress, and i hope you might find it helpful too.

  • AGI (Artificial General Intelligence) - An AI system that is capable of autonomously performing all economically valuable labor (even if doing so is not legal, economically viable, or socially acceptable)
  • ASI (Artificial Super Intelligence) - An AI system with intelligence exceeding that of the best human experts in multiple disparate fields.
    • AGI is not a prerequisite for ASI
  • Singularity - A point in time where the world is changing so rapidly that no human can reliably make accurate predictions about next week.
    • Different fields will reach the singularity at different points; We may be in a mathematics singularity right now.
  • Alignment - The process of imbuing an AI system with values considered desirable by the aligners.
  • Successionism - The belief that AIs should be a successor species to humans, potentially treating humans like how we treat our elderly or pets today.
    • There is an alternate belief that AIs should uplift human intelligence so that humans can be intellectual equals to ASI; However, I'm not yet aware of a term for this.
  • Doom - A scenario where most humans are either killed or have their living standards irrecoverably and substantially reduced.
  • Doomer - An individual who believes P(doom | current rate of AI progress) is unacceptably high
    • Usually believes P(doom | AI pause) < P(doom | current rate of AI progress)
  • Bloomer - An individual who believes P(doom | current rate of AI progress) < P(doom | world without AI progress)
