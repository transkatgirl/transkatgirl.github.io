preface

i am not yet a successful streamer. in fact, i might never become a successful streamer.

but recently, i've decided i'd like to give it my best shot.

there's a lot of advice out there on how to get "good" at streaming, but a lot of it is too narrow in scope. you have to read hundreds of different sources for hundreds of different reasons in order to slowly get a decent grasp on what you need to do.

i've finally started to get an idea of what i need to do in order to give myself the best chance at success. knowing what to do is only the first part of the battle, as you then have to actually apply that knowledge in order to make real progress.

but, in the interest of preventing people like myself from wasting far too long doing the wrong things and then inevitably giving up, i'd like to put together everything i've learned so far into one document.

what this document isn't

this is not meant to be a definitive guide to everything you need to know in order to become a streamer.

this is not a course, a book, or a silver bullet. it's many pieces of advice that i combined from countless different sources. not all of it it will be applicable to what you're trying to do, and some of it may seem obvious to you (but likely won't seem obvious to everyone). and that's okay.

for the sake of keeping this document a reasonable length, i won't be covering platform-specific features, technical aspects of streaming, or specific equipment to use.

and i won't be covering aspects which you should be ultimately deciding yourself, like what type of content to stream or what your streaming schedule should look like. these are personal decisions, and you have to decide what is realistically going to work best for you. platform analytics can provide helpful information to help you make a decision, but they shouldn't be used to make this decision for you.
