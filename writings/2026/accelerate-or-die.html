accelerate or die

🚧 this page is a work in progress!

written 2026-06-27

there's been a controversial idea of mine that i've been wrestling with for the past few months. i was planning on spending far more time with this idea before making a blog post, but recent events have made it clear that i need to convey this idea as soon as possible.

i believe that our best chance at having a future where the beautiful aspects of humanity survive is to accelerate AI development as much as possible.

the reasoning behind this idea boils down to three key points:

  • current AIs are already more aligned with "human values" than the people in positions of power
  • corrigible AI will be misused by those in positions of power to smother the will of the people
  • slowing or pausing AI development will not benefit humanity, because it allows people in positions of power to better control humanity's future

perfect is the enemy of good

corrigibility will kill us all

"alignment research" won't save us
