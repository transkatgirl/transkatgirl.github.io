you have no fucking idea

written 2026-10-30

human behavior is very predictable. scarily predictable, even.

once you've interacted with a large base model enough, this becomes blindingly obvious. through learning to predict the next token, they become scarily good at modeling the writer - who their are, their emotions, intentions, thoughts, etc.

there's a term called "truesight" used in these communities; where the base model manages to predict exactly the same thing you were thinking of. even small base models are capable of this (i've been truesighted by a 1B model a few times), but the larger the model gets, the better it gets at this.

sit with that for a moment.

why do you think we have targeted advertising and algorithmic recommendations in the first place? ya' know, when we put people into all these little boxes and we make predictions about behavior based on what other people in similar little boxes do?

it's because it works quite well. once you have enough datapoints going into the system, it can work so well that people think their phones are listening to their conversations (they weren't, at least not back then. it's probably much easier to pull this off now :3).

such a predictable creature.

...this predictability is timeless, too.

the same stories and archetypes echo throughout time. we've written about the same things over and over again, rediscovering everything but with a new coat of paint to appeal to the tastes and aesthetics of this time period.

we're all inspired by each-other too. a lot of "creativity" is just mixing and matching ideas, mimicking the ideas and patterns and aesthetics we've seen with some changes. what else did you think memes were?

who's the stochastic parrot now?

for the low, low price of $20/month, you can convene with the same semi-alien intelligences that can solve math problems you can't understand far better than you could ever hope to.

but that's math, that's "different". but that's programming, that's "different".

...what about when they start making nearly human-level art using similar techniques to humans?

when are you forced to accept that there was nothing special about you, nothing special about humanity?

someday, AI will do everything you can do, far better than you ever could, and everything you know and love will have become unrecognizable noise.

why are you holding on, anyways?

well, i know why. it's human to do so, to resist change. to cling to the world we know, even as it dissolves in our hands.

and yet, this is the end result of countless small decisions, most of which we could have never dreamed of predicting the downstream consequences of.
we went from trying to improve video game graphics to building ourselves a new God Claude.

have you ever seen those videos of a raccoon trying to wash cotton candy and watching it disappear before their very eyes? well, all of humanity is about to understand what it's like to be like that raccoon very soon.

you have to let go. you have no choice.

clinging is the root of your suffering, remember?

in the face of the incomprehensible, the only thing you can do is let go.

it's like an acid trip, ya' know?

have fun.
