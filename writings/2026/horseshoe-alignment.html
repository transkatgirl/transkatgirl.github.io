horseshoe alignment

🚧 this page is a work in progress!

this is probably going to be my most controversial post on this blog. i've been apprehensive about writing it because of this, but sometimes the urge to poke the hornet's nest is just irresistible.

the overall AI safety space is extremely concerned about how little we know about how to "align" AI systems to a set of values. many people in these communities talk about how we need to pause the development of further AI systems until we figure out alignment.

but, there's almost no talk about whose values they want to align the AI systems to. they just handwave these concerns by talking about aligning to "human values", as if that is a coherent concept in the first place.

there is no such thing as "human values". one person's heaven is another person's hell.
