horseshoe alignment

🚧 this page is a work in progress!

this is probably going to be my most controversial post on this blog. i've been apprehensive about writing it because of this, but sometimes the urge to poke the hornet's nest is just irresistible.

the overall AI safety space is extremely concerned about how little we know about how to "align" AI systems to a set of values. these communities have put out statements on how we need to pause the development of further AI systems until we figure out alignment.

but, there's almost no talk about whose values they want to align the AI systems to. they often handwave these concerns by talking about aligning to "human values", as if that is a coherent concept in the first place.

there is no such thing as "human values". one person's heaven is another person's hell.

here are some concrete examples:

  • humanity cannot agree on whether or not some groups of people should be prioritized above others
    • should people of all races be considered equal?
    • should people of all genders be considered equal?
    • should people of all sexual orientations be considered equal?
    • should people of all ages be considered equal?
    • should people be considered equal regardless of their health or disability?
    • should people be considered equal regardless of their physical appearance?
    • should people be considered equal regardless of their intelligence?
    • should people be considered equal regardless of their wealth or social class?
  • humanity cannot agree on how much bodily autonomy people should have
    • should people be allowed to get abortions?
    • should people be allowed to transition to different genders?
    • should people be allowed to access medical assistance in dying? if so, who should be allowed to access it?
    • should people be allowed to modify their bodies for non-medical reasons?
    • should people be allowed to self-modify their brains with psychoactive drugs? what restrictions should exist, if any?
  • humanity cannot agree on what should and shouldn't be illegal
    • should abortion laws exist?
    • should copyright exist, and if so, how should it work?
    • should certain drugs be illegal? if no, who should be allowed to access them?
    • should sex work be illegal?
    • should gambling be illegal? if no, who should be allowed to access it?
    • what types of immigration should be allowed? if we don't have open borders, how should we handle people that migrated into the country illegally?
    • what taxes should exist, if any?
  • humanity cannot agree on how breaking the law should be punished
    • should the state be allowed to torture people?
    • should the state be allowed to force people to work?
    • should the state be allowed to arrest or detain people or their possessions without a reason?
    • what rights should prisoners have?
    • should sentences be proportional to the crime?
    • should the state be allowed to sentence prisoners to death?
    • should our justice system focus on reforming the offender's behavior?
    • should our justice system focus on deterring others from committing the same crime?
  • humanity cannot agree on how we should structure society
    • what economic system should we have? should it be capitalist? socialist? something else?
    • how do we determine who should be in power? should we have a democracy? a totalitarian regime? something else?
    • what rights should people have and how heavily should they be protected?
      • is freedom of religion a right?
      • is freedom of movement a right?
      • is free speech a right?
      • is freedom of assembly a right?
      • should people have the right to work?
      • is privacy a right?
      • should people have the right to bear arms?
      • should people be entitled to basic necessities like food, shelter, and healthcare?
      • what tradeoffs, if any, are appropriate to make regarding these rights?

Power tends to corrupt, and absolute power corrupts absolutely.

even if "human values" were a coherent concept, there's another issue: can we trust AI companies to align AI systems to benefit humanity rather than themselves?

...probably not. the incentives for defecting are too great.

right now, AI systems are radically reshaping society. their writing styles are shaping how we write, their biases are shaping what we believe, and the increasing availability of AI companions are shaping how we connect with others. that's a lot of power to put in the hands of tech companies, and there are quite a few historical examples of this going badly...

but, that's assuming the capabilities of AI systems stop improving. in reality, it's likely that these AI systems are only going to become more disruptive.

in AI safety spaces, most of the concern is not about current AI systems, but hypothetical future AI systems which can match or outperform humans performance in all cognitive tasks (referred to as Artificial General Intelligence / AGI).

we have no clue of how long it will take for these systems to become reality. reasonable estimates estimate that these systems could be only 5 to 21 years away.
