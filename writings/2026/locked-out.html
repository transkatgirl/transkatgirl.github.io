i want to preface this all by saying that i am not a leftist, somehow.

i do not believe that ✨getting rid of capitalism✨ will just magically fix things. even with their flaws, markets actually do a pretty good job at appropriately allocating resources, and i don't want to advocate for a system that would drastically lower living standards for most people just because it might make the small proportion of people like me better off.

but man, the situation i'm in really fucking sucks, and i don't think there's much of a way out for me.
