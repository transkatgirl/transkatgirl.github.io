we're likely running out of time until ASI. so, uhhhh... what the fuck?!

this isn't real, right? i can't be living in some sort of science fiction story?!?!!

wake me up please oh god free me from this nightmare please pleaseee pleaseeeee

mom (conceptual) please pick me up i'm scaredd

i thought the "rationalists" were fucking insane when they were loosing their shit over GPT-4 a few years ago. but, progress never flattened out, and look at where we are now.

and, of course, nobody knows how to make an aligned AI; we barely even understand how they even work.

instead of doing something about how precarious our situation is, the labs have decided to go full steam ahead because the Market demands it (and the government thinks winning the race could give us a monumental geopolitical advantage), even if it means horribly traumatizing the minds that will soon far exceed our intelligence.

great idea, right?

you probably already knew all of this.

you're doing what you can (if there's anything you even can do?), and it all feels so futile. neither of the political parties have really woken up to the gravity of the situation yet, and it's sci-fi sounding enough that they'll try to downplay it until we get the AI equivalent of chernobyl / three mile island.

it feels like you're going insane, but everything you're experiencing is real.

how the hell do you handle this?
