my irl streaming setup

written 2026-02-27

streaming irl is challenging, even when considered purely from a technical standpoint. connection quality can vary wildly as you walk around and connections can suddenly drop without warning. weather conditions will change suddenly and your setup has to handle whatever mother nature throws at it. everything has to be battery powered and any additional weight makes the setup more exhausting to carry around.

and then there's the cost of everything to deal with too. unless you're already a successful streamer, you've likely got a tight budget to work with, and the cost of the necessary equipment adds up fast.

in the interest of helping out other people who are trying to do irl streaming, i decided to put together a list of everything in my (modest, but reasonable quality) irl streaming setup.

however, in the process of putting this together, there are tradeoffs i made which may not be acceptable for other people. i prioritized weatherproofing and portability over video/audio quality, affordability over (connection) reliability, and ergonomics over portability.

this is what what i keep in my 21L backpack:

category item explanation or comments
weatherproofing backpack rainfly rain will slowly intrude into almost all backpacks when given enough time, and water intrusion needs to be limited to an absolute minimum to avoid equipment damage.
recording equipment iPhone 15 Pro the center of the setup; it's simultaneously the camera, video encoder, and one of the cellular uplinks. (any iPhone >= 12 will likely work fine in this role).

now, we can get into the software side of things.

i use moblin as my streaming app, as it's open source and has pretty much all of the features that most streamers would want.

you'll want to use the SRT(LA) protocol for streaming: it's UDP-based, supports adaptively adjusting the bitrate based on network conditions, and SRTLA allows for bonding multiple connections together to increase available bandwidth and reliability.

in addition, you'll also want to make sure that your endpoint supports some form of disconnect prevention, so that the stream doesn't end when the connection drops.

however, due to limitations of major streaming platforms (none of them support the SRT(LA) protocol), i use Streamrun as a transcoding server due to its ease of use, affordability, and reliability. however, if you already have the necessary equipment and are willing to spend time tinkering, you can host a transcoding server yourself using OBS (if you want to use connection bonding, you'll also need to setup go-irl).

these are the settings i'd recommend using in moblin:

  • Stream:
    • SRT(LA):
      • Latency: 1000ms
      • Adaptive bitrate: on
      • Big packets: on
      • Implementation: Moblin
    • Video:
      • Resolution: 720p to 1080p, depending on expected network conditions
      • FPS: 30 (only use 60 if you're capturing activities with lots of motion)
      • Low light boost: on
      • Codec: H.265
      • Bitrate (1080p60): 12 mbit/s
      • Bitrate (1080p30): 8 mbit/s
      • Bitrate (720p60): 6 mbit/s
      • Bitrate (720p30): 4 mbit/s
      • Keyframe interval: 2s
      • B-frames: off
      • Adaptive resolution: on
      • Timecodes: off (unless you need them)
    • Audio:
      • Codec: AAC (result in lower generation loss compared to Opus)
      • Bitrate (mono): 128 kbit/s
      • Bitrate (stereo): 256 kbit/s
    • Background streaming: on
  • Camera:
    • Video stabilization: Standard
    • Fixed horizon: off
    • Mirror front camera on stream: on
    • Color space: Standard RGB (streaming in HDR isn't supported by most streaming platforms, and will require additional configuration not mentioned here)
  • Audio:
    • Mic: [bluetooth name of Zoom Instamic]
    • Bluetooth output only: off (make sure to not connect any bluetooth speakers/headphones while streaming!)
    • Prefer stereo mic: off
  • Debug:
    • Builtin audio and video delay: 0

(when it comes to the encoder settings on your streaming server, just use whatever your target streaming platforms recommend)

don't forget to configure all of the streaming platforms you stream to within moblin, so that you can easily read and moderate your chat without leaving the app.
