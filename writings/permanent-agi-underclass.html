i do not want to live in a world with a permanent underclass

AGI inequality is an existential threat.

🚧 this page is a work in progress!

at some point in the future, i would like to revisit this topic in order to write a more detailed and higher quality piece.

however, i've recently realized that the urgency of the situation (probable AGI timelines vary a lot, but the lower bound is concerningly soon) calls for me to write something today rather than years from now.

in the circles i'm in, there's a lot of talk about AGI[1] creating a permanent underclass.

as the idea goes, AGI will make capital far more important than it is today while making almost[2] all human labor no longer economically useful. without radical intervention, AGI will split the world into a caste system determined by how much wealth you had in the pre-AGI era.

even if equalizing measures are taken by governments, AGI will make any corruption far more dangerous, as it acts as a force multiplier for people in positions of power to hijack systems for their own benefit. in a worst case scenario, we could end up with one person controlling the fate of all of humanity.

Power tends to corrupt, and absolute power corrupts absolutely.

once the transition away from human labor is complete, there will be no incentive for those in positions of power to care about the permanent underclass, creating the possibility of an autogenocide driven purely by greed.

the "best" case scenario, where everyone ends up being provided with livable UBI by a democratic government until the end of time, won't be stable. a lack of upwards mobility in society will likely lead to political instability, and an opportunistic actor could take advantage of the power vacuum created in order to enrich themselves.

we need to start thinking about how to tackle the societal issues that AGI will create today, so that we will be prepared to face them tomorrow. failing to do so poses an existential threat to humanity[3].

a call for action

footnotes

[1] in the context of this piece, AGI (Artificial General Intelligence) refers to a future AI system or set of systems capable of performing all economically valuable human labor.

[2] even in a post-AGI world, there will likely be a small number of humans employed to serve people in the upper classes.

[3] a small group of rich elites who screwed over everybody else so that they could conquer the universe does not count as "humanity". i am not willing to debate this.
